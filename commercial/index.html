Restaurant Services

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

Restaurants and food-service businesses have climate demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Kitchens generate enormous heat loads while dining rooms need to stay comfortable, exhaust hoods pull out conditioned air that makeup air units have to replace, and a busy Friday night pushes rooftop units to their limit. Poinciana HVAC specializes in food-service HVAC, including rooftop package units, split systems, correct tonnage and airflow sizing, makeup air balancing against hood exhaust, and scheduled service that works around your hours.

Air balance is where many restaurant HVAC problems start. When a kitchen runs under negative pressure, it pulls dining-room air the wrong way, sucks hot or cold outdoor air in through every door, and wears out your staff at peak service. Our technicians balance the makeup air to the hood exhaust and log every visit in written reports your landlord and auditors can review.

Equipment replacements and maintenance schedules are planned with owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits the hours you're open. When a job can't happen during service, we install during off-hours, keeping your kitchen cooking and the dining room comfortable in the meantime.

Inspections & Documentation

Professional Installation and Documentation

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. Our technicians inspect heat exchangers for cracks, verify proper venting and combustion air, test electrical connections and disconnects, document the refrigerant charge, clear condensate lines, and check ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is simple: protecting the people in your building from CO hazards, poor air quality, and water damage. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. Every account gets a service binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, giving you proof of due diligence for landlords, insurers, and auditors. Equipment that works hardest, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, is put on a more frequent visit schedule.

Rapid Response

Rapid-Response HVAC Repair for Businesses

Each hour without climate control costs a business money. For a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. In a retail store, it empties the aisles on a summer afternoon. For a server room, it risks overheated equipment, and in a leased building it leads to tenant disputes. Poinciana HVAC offers fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties across Poinciana and surrounding areas, dispatching an experienced technician quickly when you call.
